About My Dashboard

My Dashboard is a simple and user-friendly learning platform designed to help students improve their knowledge and develop new skills in web development. Our goal is to make learning easy, organized and accessible for everyone.

We provide different courses including HTML, CSS and JavaScript. These courses help beginners understand the basics of website development and learn how to create attractive and interactive web pages.

Our platform allows students to explore courses, manage their learning activities and keep track of their progress. Students can also access their assignments and organize their learning journey in one place.

Whether you are a beginner or want to improve your existing skills, My Dashboard provides a simple environment to support your learning journey. We believe that learning becomes easier when information is well organized and presented clearly.
